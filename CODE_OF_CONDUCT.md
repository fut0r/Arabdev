# Code of conduct

ArabDev is built by people who give their time to it. To keep it a place people want to come back
to, everyone taking part in the project (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, email) agrees
to the following.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Help newcomers. Everyone asked a first question once.
- Write in Arabic or English, whichever you're comfortable with.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Discrimination or hateful remarks about anyone's origin, religion, gender, disability or anything
  else about who they are.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Reporting

Write to **support@arabdev.site** with what happened and links if you have them. Reports are read
by the maintainers and kept confidential. If the report concerns a maintainer, say so in the
subject and the others will handle it.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide what to do based on what happened: a private word, a public warning, a temporary
ban from the repository, or a permanent one. People using arabdev.site itself are also bound by the
[community guidelines](https://wiki.arabdev.site/en/#community-guidelines).
